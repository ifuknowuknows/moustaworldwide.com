The vision

We want the same things. We just show it differently.

To belong. To be safe. To be taken seriously. Culture changes how we say it, and when we forget that, we stop seeing each other as people.

I've seen where that leads. In Iraq I listened to Syrian refugees, and in Rwanda to survivors of the 1994 genocide against the Tutsi. So I build things that put people in the same room.

Writing

AI literacy

AI will change the world. Everyone should understand it.

I've spent years nerding out on AI: how it works, what it can do, and how it can be used for us and against us.

Right now, understanding AI is a privilege. I want to make it common. More AI literacy, in plain words, for everyone, so more people get a say in what comes next.

Short bio for press and events

Mousta M. Moustafa is a Danish lawyer, founder and former board member of Amnesty International Denmark. In 2017, at 26, he became Denmark's youngest lawyer. Since 2014 he has helped start-ups and scale-ups build and operate across the world, closing deals in more than 50 countries. He speaks eight languages, has visited more than 100 countries, sits on the board of Chapters Institute and has given two TEDx talks.
