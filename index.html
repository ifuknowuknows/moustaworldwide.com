The short version

Forget becoming a lawyer.
Qualified at 26.

At school in Herlev I was the troublemaker, and a counsellor told me to forget about law. In 2017 I qualified as an attorney at 26, widely reported as Denmark's youngest lawyer at the time.

Since then I've learned that a title doesn't answer the bigger questions about who you are and where you belong. Eight languages and a hundred-odd countries taught me more about that than any job did.

Now I build things: a language app, a book, a life with more room in it. I share the journey while it's happening, not after.
