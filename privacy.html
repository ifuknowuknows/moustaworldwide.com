Privacy

What this site knows about you

Short version: almost nothing. Last updated 24 November 2026.

No cookies, no tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, ads or tracking pixels.

Your language choice

If you pick a language, your browser remembers it in its own local storage so the site opens in that language next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to me. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Services that deliver the page

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts and map data load from jsDelivr. Like any web server, these services receive your IP address and browser details to deliver the files, under their own privacy policies.

If you email me

I use your email address and message only to reply to you.
